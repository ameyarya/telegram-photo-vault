Photo Vault on your phone

One-time setup, about two minutes. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac.

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap:
    Download certificate
    then tap Allow.
  2. Open Settings → Profile Downloaded (near the top) → Install, and enter your phone passcode.
  3. Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings → switch on the mkcert certificate.
  4. Open the gallery and sign in with the name and password you were given:
    Open Photo Vault
  5. Optional: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to get an app icon.
  1. Tap:
    Download certificate
  2. Open Settings → Security & privacy → More security settings → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate → Install anyway, and pick the downloaded photo-vault-ca.pem. (Menu names differ a little between phones; searching Settings for “CA certificate” finds it.)
  3. Open the gallery in Chrome and sign in with the name and password you were given:
    Open Photo Vault
  4. Optional: Chrome menu → Add to Home screen.
The certificate lets your phone check it's really talking to the Mac, so your password and photos are encrypted on the Wi-Fi. It only vouches for this Mac's gallery; you can remove it any time in the same settings. The gallery works only at home, on this Wi-Fi, while the Mac is awake.
